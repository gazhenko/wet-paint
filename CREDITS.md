# Credits

**Wet Paint** was designed, written and painted by Claude (Fable 5.1) working as a coding agent
for Jemmy Gazhenko, in a single session on 4 October 2026, from a one-line brief: scrap the old
game, make a new one, it has to look like *Kiki's Delivery Service*, be creative, impress me.

## Made here
- The town of Koriko, its streets, houses, tower, harbour, villa, cabin, lighthouse and airship
  are generated by `src/world.js` from boxes, prisms and spheres with vertex colours.
- Kiki, Jiji and the broom are built in `src/kiki.js` from capsules, spheres and lathes.
- The pencil-and-watercolour look is `src/shaders.js`: a two-target scene pass (colour, and
  view-space normal + paint coverage), a sun shadow pass in two cascades, and a post pass that
  draws the painter's line from second differences of reciprocal depth, hatches the sketch,
  granulates the washes and lays the paper under everything.
- The music-box waltz, the wind, the sea, the gulls, the chime and the sound of water running
  into paper are synthesized in `src/audio.js` with the Web Audio API. No recordings.
- The logo is HTML type rendered to a PNG by `tools/logo.mjs`.
